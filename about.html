About Us

Our mission

MedReach SL is a Digital Public Good built to close the healthcare information gap for Sierra Leoneans, regardless of where they live or what device they use.

Who We Are

Built by and for Sierra Leoneans

MedReach SL began as a student project addressing a real societal problem: many Sierra Leoneans, especially in rural areas, struggle to know which clinic to visit, whether essential medicines are in stock, or how to reach a trusted community health worker. We built a free, lightweight, mobile-first platform to solve exactly that.

🎯

Our Goal

Connect every Sierra Leonean — urban or rural — to the nearest clinic, available medicines, and a trusted health worker, regardless of internet speed or device.

🌍

Open & Free

MedReach SL is free to use and built on open data principles, in line with Digital Public Good standards.

🤝

Community-Driven

Data on clinics, medicine stock, and CHW availability is informed by community reporting and government health facility records.

SDG Alignment

Supporting global goals, locally

SDG 3

Good Health & Well-Being

Bridging the gap between patients and care providers in underserved areas across Sierra Leone.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

Ensuring rural and urban populations have equal access to health information and services.

SDG 17

Partnerships for the Goals

Built with open data shared across government, NGOs, and communities across the country.
