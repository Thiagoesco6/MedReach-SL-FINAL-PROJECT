Core Features

Built for real conditions in Sierra Leone

Designed to work on 2G networks, feature phones, and shared devices across urban and rural areas.

🏥

Clinic Locator

Find the nearest government and NGO health facilities with live distance information and service availability.

💊

Medicine Availability

Real-time stock status for essential medicines at local clinics and health posts across all districts.

👩‍⚕️

CHW Directory

Connect with verified community health workers serving your district for personalised guidance.

📶

Works Offline

Core features function without internet — critical data is cached locally on your device for areas with poor connectivity.

📱

Any Device

Fully responsive across smartphones, feature phones, tablets, and desktops — no app download required.

🔒

Privacy First

Your health data stays private. No account required to search for clinics or check medicine availability.

How It Works

Simple. Fast. Accessible.

Four steps to getting the healthcare help you need — no registration required to search.

1

Enter your location

Type your district or allow location access on your device to find services near you.

2

Browse clinics & medicines

See what's nearby, what's in stock, and which community health workers are available.

3

Connect with a CHW

Reach a verified health worker in your district for personalised, trusted guidance.

4

Get care

Visit the right clinic at the right time, with the right information already in hand.
