Health News & Alerts

Stay informed

The latest health advisories, disease outbreak alerts, and platform updates for Sierra Leone.

Alert
June 2026 · Bo & Kenema Districts

Increased malaria cases reported during rainy season

Health authorities report a rise in malaria cases in Bo and Kenema. Residents are advised to use treated mosquito nets and seek early treatment at the nearest clinic if symptoms occur.

Platform Update
June 2026

Medicine Stock Tracker now covers all 14 districts

MedReach SL has expanded its medicine availability data to cover government and community health facilities in every district across the country.

Health Tip
May 2026

Safe water practices to prevent cholera

Boil or chlorinate drinking water, wash hands with soap regularly, and store water in clean covered containers to reduce the risk of cholera and other waterborne diseases.

Alert
May 2026 · Bombali District

TB drug shortage at Makeni Regional Hospital — alternatives available

A temporary shortage of select TB medications has been reported. Patients are advised to check the Medicine Tracker for nearby facilities with available stock.

Platform Update
April 2026

New CHWs onboarded in Pujehun and Kailahun

Two new community health workers have joined the MedReach SL network, expanding coverage in Pujehun and Kailahun districts.

Health Tip
April 2026

Routine immunization keeps children protected

Parents are encouraged to keep up with routine EPI vaccinations for children under five. Check the Medicine Tracker to confirm vaccine availability at your nearest clinic.
