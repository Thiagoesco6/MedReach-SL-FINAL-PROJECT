Privacy Policy

MedReach SL is built with privacy as a core principle. We collect the minimum information necessary to provide our services:

  • Information you provide voluntarily when registering (name, email, phone, district)
  • Messages sent to community health workers through the Contact page
  • General usage data to improve clinic and medicine information accuracy

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes. Clinic searches and medicine availability checks can be performed without creating an account.

Data Storage

Core information such as clinic locations, medicine availability, and CHW directories is cached locally on your device to allow the platform to function with limited or no internet connectivity.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at info@medreachsl.org.
